Everyday caregiving with Cerebral Palsy

Mobility, movement, expression, and daily support needs may change with circumstances and activities.

The Cerebral Palsy journal covers movement, mobility, communication, and everyday support needs. Caregivers can choose which observations are worth recording, or simply use the educational information without writing entries.

What’s included

Condition-specific learning resources and safety checklists, with optional journaling to help keep observations organized.

Entries are stored locally in an encrypted database on your device. The app works offline for everyday use, requires no account, and offers an optional App Lock. Internet access is required for purchases and certain optional actions.

This journal is included at no cost. Caregiver Plus is an optional one-time upgrade for Appointment Reports and Trends & Charts across all journals. No subscription required.
