Privacy Policy for Dementia Journal

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Dementia Journal is designed with your privacy as the top priority.

Data Collection

Dementia Journal does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All journal entries, notes, and settings are stored locally on your device only, in an encrypted database. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party.

Data Storage

Your data stays on your device. If you use the app's Export Database Backup feature, the backup file is saved locally on your device and is only shared if you choose to share it yourself (e.g. via email or file transfer).

Permissions

The app requests the biometric (fingerprint/face) permission solely to let you lock the app with your device's screen lock. This is handled entirely by your device's operating system — we never receive or store any biometric data.

Third-Party Services

Dementia Journal does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

It does contain optional links to external websites. If you follow one, you leave the app and that website's own privacy policy applies to what you do there.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 18.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted here with a revised date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to jason@mycarejournals.com.
