Everyday caregiving with Generalized Anxiety Disorder

Generalized anxiety disorder involves persistent worry that can affect sleep, concentration, physical comfort, and everyday routines.

Support works best when it respects the person’s preferences, avoids judgment, and follows any agreed professional care plan.

Changes in tension, energy, sleep, or participation can have many possible causes; a journal can organize observations without diagnosing anxiety or replacing professional assessment.

Nine journal categories

The journal offers optional prompts for observations in these nine areas:

  • Worry Patterns
  • Stressful Situations
  • Body Sensations
  • Sleep and Energy
  • Focus and Decisions
  • Daily Participation
  • Connection and Support
  • Treatment and Coping
  • Other
