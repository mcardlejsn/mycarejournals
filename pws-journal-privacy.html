Privacy Policy for PWS Journal

Last updated: August 11, 2026

PWS Journal is a private journal for family caregivers. It runs entirely on your device.

Data Collection

PWS Journal does not collect personal data. There are no accounts, analytics, advertisements, or tracking. The developer cannot see anything you enter. The app does not automatically upload or transmit your journal information.

Data Storage

Journal entries, categories, dates, notes, and settings are stored locally in an encrypted database on your device.

Local Backups

You can create encrypted local backups from the Data Management screen. The app retains up to five local backups in its private storage. When a new backup exceeds that limit, the oldest local backup is automatically deleted. You can also manually delete any listed backup.

Saving and Importing Backup Copies

Save a Copy opens your device's system file picker and lets you choose where an encrypted backup copy is saved, such as Downloads, Documents, removable storage, or another available storage provider. PWS Journal does not email the file or open the system Share menu.

If you save a copy to cloud storage or later send it somewhere, that copy is handled by the service you selected and its privacy policy applies. A backup may contain your complete journal history, so treat it as a private care record.

Import Backup File lets you select an encrypted PWS Journal backup and restore its contents. The selected file is read for the restore and is not sent to the developer.

App Lock

If you enable App Lock, PWS Journal asks your device's operating system to verify your enrolled fingerprint or face before opening the app. PWS Journal never receives or stores your biometric information.

Guidance and Reference Information

The app includes general caregiver guidance and reference information. Viewing this information does not contact a server or send information about you or the person you support.

Third-Party Services

PWS Journal contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. It contains optional links to external websites. If you follow one, you leave the app and that website's privacy policy applies.

Deleting Your Information

Uninstalling PWS Journal removes its database and app-managed local backups. Backup copies that you saved to Downloads, Documents, a computer, removable storage, cloud storage, or another location are not removed. You must delete those copies yourself.

Children's Privacy

This app is intended for caregivers and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted here with a revised date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to jason@mycarejournals.com.
