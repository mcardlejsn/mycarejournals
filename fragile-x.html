Everyday caregiving with Fragile X

Sensory needs, expression, behavior, and familiar routines can influence day-to-day comfort and support.

The Fragile X journal covers communication, sensory needs, behavior, and daily routines. Caregivers can choose which observations are worth recording, or simply use the educational information without writing entries.

What’s included

Condition-specific learning resources and safety checklists, with optional journaling to help keep observations organized.

Entries are stored locally in an encrypted database on your device. The app works offline for everyday use, requires no account, and offers an optional App Lock. Internet access is required for purchases and certain optional actions.

This journal is an optional $4.99 one-time purchase on Android (US pricing). Unlock All is also available as a one-time purchase covering current and future paid journals. No subscription required.
