Optional advanced tools

Meet Caregiver Plus.

All journals include educational guidance, safety checklists, and unlimited observations at no cost. Caregiver Plus adds two optional ways to organize and review what you've recorded.

Appointment Reports

Create print-friendly PDF reports from your recorded observations. Choose a date range, then preview, save, share, or print for appointments.

Trends & Charts

Explore recorded observations over time and by journal category. Charts reflect what you've logged, not medically verified symptom frequency.

Caregiver Plus — $9.99 one-time

No subscription. Unlock both tools across current and future journals. Tools work offline after activation; purchase and restoration require internet access. Store prices may vary by region.

Built with real care experience

Thoughtful tools for everyday caregiving.

My Care Journals was created by an independent developer in Maine who also works directly in caregiving. The goal is simple: help people organize observations, find useful information, and keep personal notes private.

No app replaces professional guidance or the people providing care. But it can make important details easier to remember.
